Hands-On Exercise • 5 Minutes

The Reverse Brand Sprint

Do not brand yourself yet. Brand an iconic subject first.

Option A: A Parent

Distill their core superpower and catchphrase.

Option B: A Superhero

Batman, Iron Man, Storm—define their market brand.

Option C: A Historical Legend

Da Vinci, Cleopatra, Churchill—what is their proof?

Sprint Template • 5 Minutes

Answer These 3 Prompts (5 Mins)

Step 1

The Tagline

Describe them in one punchy sentence.

Ex: “The nocturnal detective for Gotham.”
Step 2

The Signature Signal

What is their unmistakable visual or voice?

Ex: “The bat signal in dark sky.”
Step 3

The Irrefutable Proof

What indisputable deed validates them?

Ex: “Arkham is locked every night.”
